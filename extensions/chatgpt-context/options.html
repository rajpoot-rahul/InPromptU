INPROMPTU COMPANION

Your conversations, in context.

Pair with InPromptU to automatically share loaded user and assistant messages from saved ChatGPT conversations you open. Collection continues while the extension is paired and the desktop bridge is on.

This does not read unopened chats, attachments, account credentials or hidden agent state. The desktop app keeps collected messages in memory. When you improve a prompt, selected excerpts may be sent to the AI provider you selected in InPromptU.

Checking connection…

Pair again after restarting InPromptU or reconnecting its bridge. Disconnect the source in InPromptU to clear collected messages. Use the extension only with conversations you intend to share.
