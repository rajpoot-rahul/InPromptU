                  BEFORE YOU TURN IT ON
                  Your screen, your call
                  
    
                  When the mascot is on, Impromptu can read data from your screen. It only does so when you click one of its actions.
                  
    
                  It can
                  • Copy the text you have selected in any app, when you choose Improve or Send
                  • Capture what is visible on your screen when you choose Explain, Ask or Read this page
                  • Press Page Down in the front window to read a long page, then scroll back
                  • Nudge you about long prompting sessions and repeated prompts, using only your activity inside Impromptu
                  It never
                  • Records continuously or in the background
                  • Watches your typing or other apps
                  • Sends screenshots to a cloud model: screen reading uses only a local Ollama model
                  • Saves screenshots. Only the text answer is kept, in Local chat, if saving is on
                  
    
                  A red dot and a “Reading your screen” message appear whenever it captures. Turn the mascot off at any time in Settings. On macOS it needs Accessibility and Screen Recording permission, which you grant in System Settings.
