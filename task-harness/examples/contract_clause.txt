SERVICES AGREEMENT - CLAUSE 9: TERM, RENEWAL AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement starts on 1 February 2026 and continues for an initial term of 24 months.

9.2 After the initial term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods,
unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the
current term.

9.3 The Customer may terminate for convenience on 60 days' written notice, provided that the
Customer pays an early termination fee equal to 3 months of the then-current monthly fee.

9.4 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party materially breaches this Agreement
and fails to remedy the breach within 30 days of written notice.

9.5 The monthly fee during the initial term is USD 18,500. Any increase on renewal is capped at 5%.

Parties: Northwind Analytics Ltd ("Supplier") and Lakeside Health Group ("Customer").
