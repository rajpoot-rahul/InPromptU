Remote Work Policy (excerpt) - effective 1 January 2027

1. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
2. New joiners must work from the office for their first 30 days, unless their manager approves an exception in writing.
3. Remote work from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year, and requires approval from HR at least 10 working days in advance.
4. The 20-day limit does not apply to employees on an approved relocation plan.
5. Equipment: the company provides one laptop and one monitor. Additional equipment up to 150 EUR per year may be reimbursed with receipts.
6. This policy does not apply to roles that require on-site work, such as data centre and facilities staff.

Questions: contact the People Team at people@example-company.example.
