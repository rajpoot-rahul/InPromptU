Task Harness - 5-minute submission video script

[00:00:00] INTRO
I'm Surekha Jaknalli, from team Impromptu, with Rahul Rajpoot and Vivek Chaturvedi. Impromptu helps people write better prompts. My part, Task Harness, makes sure the AI's answers can be trusted.

[00:00:13] GAP
AI is usually right, but sometimes quietly wrong. In our test, the cheapest Claude model got 6 out of 30 invoices wrong, and nobody was warned. Impromptu asks the AI to keep every fact and constraint, but today nothing checks that it actually did.

[00:00:30] DEMO
Task Harness checks every AI answer with plain code: is each value really in the source, is it from the right place, do the numbers add up. If not, the AI gets one retry with the exact problem, and otherwise a person reviews it. With the harness, the cheapest model got 30 out of 30 correct, with zero silent errors, about 38 times cheaper than the biggest model.

[00:00:53] IMPROMPTU
Our next step is to plug the same gate into Impromptu, so every prompt rewrite gets checked. Nothing lost: every name, number, date and every not is kept. Nothing invented. And the chosen skill is followed. If a check fails, the AI retries once, and the user sees a clear warning instead of a silently changed prompt. Impromptu improves the question; Task Harness checks the answer.
